College Placement Analytics

Analyzed college placement data to understand placement performance and identify factors related to student outcomes.

  • Cleaned and analyzed placement data.
  • Examined branch, CGPA, skills and internship status.
  • Calculated placement percentage.
  • Calculated branch-wise average package.
  • Studied company-wise hiring.

Compared placement outcomes across branches and student attributes to identify patterns associated with placement performance.
